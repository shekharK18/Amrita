About Me

Where fabric
meets vision

I am Amrita Bhattacharjee — an innovative Fashion Designer and NIFT graduate with a passion for end-to-end design excellence. Skilled in advanced draping, print development, and the complete design lifecycle from concept to final production for both pret and couture lines.

From seasonal collection conceptualization to managing vendor relationships, fit trials, and quality assurance — I believe every garment tells a story. I specialize in print development, embroidery layouts, silhouette creation, and creative direction across editorial and commercial fashion.

Having worked with some of India's most celebrated fashion houses — Anushree Reddy, Anamika Khanna, Acute Rebellion, and Myst Designs — I bring both creative vision and production rigour to every project.
